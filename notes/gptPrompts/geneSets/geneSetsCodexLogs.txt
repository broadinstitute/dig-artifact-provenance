• Created src/python/gene_set/ingest_gene_set_prov_from_s3.py:1.

  The script:

  - Requires --in_s3_bucket and --in_sqlite_db.
  - Supports optional --in_log_file.
  - Supports optional --in_prov_file_name, defaulting to exactly geneset.provenance.dapper.yaml.
  - Deletes existing geneset rows from prov_artifact and prov_document.
  - Loads matching S3 YAML files into prov_document.
  - Loads gene sets into prov_artifact.
  - Sets prov_artifact.provenance, trait_legacy_id, and ancestry_id to NaN.
  - Skips/logs duplicate collection and gene set IDs.

  Test completed with:

  - Inserted 4 prov_document rows.
  - Inserted 10,332 prov_artifact rows.
